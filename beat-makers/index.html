Beat Makers

a cooperative musical board game

Listen to a song, then rebuild it together, one instrument each.

Open a table, share its four-letter code, and everyone who opens this page can join. Each player takes a color, draws an instrument, and places tokens on the 8 × 16 board until the whole arrangement matches.
