    Pace yourself

    Drink water between stops and eat something plain like bread or a pretzel. Acidity adds up fast, and stops 7 and 8 need a fresh palate.

    Ask off-menu

    Ask every stand "anything sour or funky that's not on the board?" Small brewers often bring a bottle of a barrel project.
