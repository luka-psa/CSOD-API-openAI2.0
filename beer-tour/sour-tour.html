    Pace yourself

    Water is €2 at the food-token stand next to the tombola. Saucisson d'Olivier (between stands 8 and 9) sells tasting boards of dried sausage, which is a good palate reset between stops.

    Ask off-menu

    Ask at stands 3, 11, 13 and 18 whether they have anything sour that isn't in the booklet. Small brewers often bring a bottle of a barrel project.
