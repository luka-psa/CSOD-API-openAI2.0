Hearth's interpreter is a fine-tuned version of FunctionGemma 270M (google/functiongemma-270m-it), a Gemma model.

Gemma is provided under and subject to the Gemma Terms of Use found at ai.google.dev/gemma/terms.
Use of this model is also subject to the Gemma Prohibited Use Policy at ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy.

Modifications: fine-tuned by the Hearth project on synthetic Dutch/English household-meeting conversations to output
Hearth actions; exported to ONNX with int4 weights for ONNX Runtime on the CPU.
